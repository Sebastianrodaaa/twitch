# Inspirations

Speed separation and learning from experts are established ideas. twitch tests online distillation from asynchronous frozen-frame judgments in a changing 30 Hz environment.

- **SwiftSage** combines a fast policy with a slow language-model planner in interactive tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17390)
- **DPT-Agent** applies dual-process control to real-time collaboration. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11882)
- **JevRSI** pairs fixed-weight System-1 and System-2 models with a harness that System-2 revises. In twitch, the slow teacher's frozen-frame judgments update the reflex weights during play. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22432)
- **ReflexGrad** routes between fast and slow systems for within-episode recovery. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14584)
- **Input prediction and mishit correction** use predicted actions to hide multimodal game latency. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17250)
- **Beveridge** provides calibrated probabilities over typed questions and is the zero-shot CPU baseline. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18655)
- **Decision 1.0** studies decision foundation models. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18629)
- **System 1 agents** explores local fast agentic decisions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21053)
- **Policy distillation** transfers an expert's action distribution, and **DAgger** collects expert labels on learner-visited states. [Policy distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06295) · [DAgger](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html)
